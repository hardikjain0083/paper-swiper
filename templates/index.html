Your personal console for exploring today’s AI & Computer Science papers. Data is fetched from CORE, stored in MongoDB, and served by your Flask backend – this page is just the front-end window.

request_today_papers.py 

from client import PapersSDK

sdk = PapersSDK(domain="cloud_computing")
today = sdk.fetch_latest()

for paper in today:
    sdk.swipe(paper)
